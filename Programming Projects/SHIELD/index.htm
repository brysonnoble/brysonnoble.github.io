Goal and motivation: Team S.H.E.I.L.D is challenged to design, build, and test a counter-UAS system for small, low-altitude aerial targets. This system shall be capable of autonomously detecting, tracking, and intercepting an aerial target using onboard sensing and computing.

Approach:

  • Detect, track, and intercept unmanned aircraft systems within 20 meters.
  • Engage the manual override SHIELD for peace of mind, or let our onboard AI do the work for you!
  • Deploy with confidence; SHIELD is FAA part 107 compliant.
